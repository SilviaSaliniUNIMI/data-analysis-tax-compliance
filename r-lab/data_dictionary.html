Municipal income snapshot

redditi.csv is the 8,047-row prepared file supplied with the original sampling example. Each row is a municipality in that historical snapshot. Its tax year is not identified in the prepared file. The separate raw IRPEF 2022 file has a different number of municipalities and is not used in this laboratory.

VariableMeaning
CodMunicipal identifier, treated as a label
Denominazione ComuneMunicipal name
ProvProvince abbreviation; missing for some rows, unused in this laboratory
RegRegion
Rp_freqRecorded frequency of pension income
Rp_totRecorded pension-income amount, EUR
Rp_avgRp_tot / Rp_freq, EUR within a municipality
Rimp_freqRecorded frequency of taxable income
Rimp_totRecorded taxable-income amount, EUR
Rp_share100 × Rp_tot / Rimp_tot, rounded municipal percentage

The mean across municipal averages gives each municipality the same weight. The aggregate amount/frequency ratio uses frequency weights.

BES education indicators

bes_education.csv derives from the supplied istruzione.xlsx. The 20-region version excludes the two autonomous-province rows while retaining their aggregate region. Selected indicators used by the script were checked against the 2020 source values. Indicator labels and their denominators concern different age ranges, so their percentages are not interchangeable.

The script uses neet_pct, early_leaving_pct, tertiary_pct, cultural_pct, reading_pct and library_pct, all recorded as percentages. Their differences are percentage points. Other columns are retained from the supplied file and are not analysed in the script.

Area mapping: Nord-Ovest → North-West; Nord-Est → North-East; Centro → Centre; Sud-Isole → South-Islands.

Derived category

dropout means early leaving above 15%, or at most 15%. This is a region-level classification chosen for the teaching exercise.

Provenance

Municipal income data and BES source files were supplied by Silvia Salini for HCAI. The sampling file is an administrative-data teaching population. The education indicators originate from Istat BES. The activities illustrate statistical calculations on those supplied snapshots.
