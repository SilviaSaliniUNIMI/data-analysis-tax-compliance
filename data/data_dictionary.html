Synthetic filings: data dictionary

24 fictional firms, each with one filing for a single reference year. No real records, legal categories, rates or national estimates. Rows were manually constructed to illustrate skewness, missingness and selection. They are not a random sample.

discrepancy_found1 = discrepancy detected, 0 = no discrepancy detected, conditional on an audit14 blanks, all unaudited firms

An observed discrepancy does not establish intention or a legal violation. No probability sampling scheme is specified. The data support descriptive calculations on these fictional observations.

Blank numeric fields are imported as NA by read.csv(). Quantiles in this activity use R type = 7. Sample variance and SD use n−1.
